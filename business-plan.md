## 1. Executive summary
An **AI-native school platform** for Indian private K-12: land with an **AI teacher's aide + student
tutor** (grading, lesson plans, 1:1 vernacular tutoring) that teachers feel value from day one, then
**expand into a sticky school ERP** (attendance, fees, comms). Wedge → expand → lock-in.

**Why now (🟢):** AI + computational thinking becomes **compulsory from Class 3, AY 2026-27** (MoE).
~70% of Indian teachers already use AI — mostly *free, unaligned* ChatGPT. NEP 2020 mandates
personalized + multilingual learning. Timing tailwind is regulatory *and* behavioral.

**Why us vs Inforida (the incumbent we target):** Inforida is a **Mar-2025 entity** with **thin, largely
unverified traction** (flagship attendance app = **100+ installs**, self-contradicting "500k vs 1.2M
students" claims) and **3 of 6 modules still in beta**. It leans on a **commission-reseller channel**
with thin economics and no proven direct B2B-education sales. The market is **wide open** — no
incumbent has real lock-in. We beat them on **depth (ship one module fully), vernacular quality,
privacy-by-design (DPDP), and founder-led direct sales + reference accounts.**

**The ask:** **$30k for 10%** ($300k valuation) → 3-month build to paying pilot schools.

## 2. Problem
- **30:1+ student-teacher ratio** — no time for personalized help. Teachers lose hours/day to
  grading, lesson prep, attendance, parent messages. 🟢
- Schools juggle **WhatsApp + paper registers + Excel + 3–4 disconnected tools.**
- Existing software is **clunky, English-first, expensive**, or (Inforida) **early/beta and unproven.**
- Free ChatGPT/Gemini fills the gap but is **not syllabus-aligned, not vernacular-tuned, not
  DPDP-compliant for schools, and has no school workflow.**

## 3. Solution & product
**Two layers, sequenced:**
1. **AI Tutor & Teacher Aide (launch first, the wedge):** auto-grading (incl. handwriting in Indian
   scripts), 30-second board-aligned lesson plans, adaptive practice, 24/7 student tutor in Hindi +
   regional languages. Immediate teacher value → internal champions.
2. **School ERP (expansion, the revenue lock-in):** attendance, fees (GST receipts, PF/ESI/TDS
   payroll), timetable, grades, parent comms. Sticky; switching cost rises per module.

**Build principle:** AI-first, one login, one database, works on **low-end Android + web**.
**Differentiator moat:** vernacular + syllabus-alignment + **DPDP Data-Processor posture** +
school-workflow depth — the four things raw ChatGPT can't provide and Inforida hasn't proven.

## 6. Business model
- **B2B SaaS, per-student-per-year**, land-and-expand. Blended anchor **₹300/student/yr** for tutor tier (🟡 test in discovery).
- Tiers: **AI Tutor** → **+ ERP add-on** → **Bundle (discounted).**
- Account value grows 2–4× as ERP modules attach; **switching cost rises with every module → low churn.**
- Undercut Inforida's per-student model on large schools with a **flat/per-teacher option**; **free attendance tier** as top-of-funnel.

## 7. Go-to-market
1. **Land** — free/low-cost AI-tutor pilot; teachers become champions.
2. **Convert** — prove outcomes (hrs saved, grading time) → paid tutor contract with the school.
3. **Expand** — upsell ERP modules; whole school locked in.
- **Channels:** direct founder-led sales to private-school **principals/owners** (economic buyer),
  **teacher as champion**, principal referrals, CBSE/school-cluster networks. Later: recruit
  Inforida's frustrated resellers with a better product/payout.
- **Beachhead:** NCR private schools. Tight reference-account playbook > broad thin reach.

## 8. Competition  *(detail in findings §2)*
- **Direct:** Inforida (target), Teachmint (distress signal — staff 790→187), LEAD (heavy/expensive), Entab/Fedena (no real AI, dated UX).
- **The real default = "free":** WhatsApp+paper + free ChatGPT (~70% teachers). **This, not Teachmint, is the competitor to beat.**
- **Our answer:** syllabus-alignment + vernacular + DPDP-compliant workflow + ERP stickiness — none of which raw ChatGPT provides.

- **Goal:** paying schools + proof the wedge→ERP motion works.

## 13. Key risks & mitigations  *(pre-mortem, findings §5)*
1. **Free ChatGPT good enough** → schools won't pay for tutor. *Mitigation: lead with ERP revenue + compliance; tutor as free wedge.*
2. **Can't reach schools cheaply** → founder sales stalls past ~50 schools. *Test CAC in discovery.*
3. **DPDP compliance cost** sinks lean team. *Strict Data-Processor posture, no child profiling.*
4. **Govt pilots eat time, no revenue.** *Exclude govt from plan.*
5. **Incumbent bundles AI free & out-distributes.** *Watch quarterly; win on depth + trust.*

## 14. Before you build (exit gates — not cleared yet)
Per methodology, **do not write production code** until ~15–20 NCR private-school interviews
(principal + teacher) close two open questions:
1. Is the paid need the **tutor** or the **ERP**?
2. **Pricing power vs free AI** — will schools actually pay ₹/student for the tutor gap?
Market is real; these two answers decide the shape of the business.

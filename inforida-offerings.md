## 2. Product catalog (6 modules)

### 2.1 Nucleus — core School ERP  *(live, the foundation)*
**Does:** admissions, fees, attendance, communication — the school OS. Target: founders, principals, teachers, parents.
**Features `[site]`:** fee automation w/ GST receipts · payroll w/ PF/ESI/TDS · parent app + WhatsApp Business · 12+ roles granular access · realtime P&L across branches · admission funnel + collections dashboard · attendance + assessment feeds · auto-flagged at-risk learners · parent-comms tracking · bulk Excel import · auto-configure classes/sections · report-card generation · 50+ built-in reports.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** this is table-stakes and mature incumbents (Entab 1,200 schools, Fedena) already do it. Beat on **UX + vernacular + price**, not feature count. Fee/payroll compliance (GST/PF/ESI/TDS) is table-stakes — must match day one.

### 2.2 Orbit AI — AI teacher + LMS  `[beta]`
**Does:** courses, lessons, AI-graded assignments, certificates, 24/7 AI tutor. Target: teachers, students, principals.
**Features `[site]`:** 4-min course builder (idea/PDF/YouTube → modules+lessons+quizzes) · public SEO course catalog · handwriting auto-grading + AI explanations · adaptive practice · verifiable certificates · 24/7 AI tutor chat · lesson plans aligned to school board · 30-sec lesson-plan generation.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** **beta** = not proven. This is the module most exposed to **free ChatGPT/Gemini** (teachers already get lesson plans free). Win only via **deep Hindi/vernacular quality + board-aligned accuracy on low-end Android**. Handwriting-grading accuracy in Indian scripts is hard and verifiable — a real differentiator if you nail it.

### 2.3 QuickEnroll AI — admissions intelligence  `[beta]`
**Does:** AI lead scoring + doc verification for admissions. Headline "3.2× fill rate." Target: admission heads, founders.
**Features `[site]`:** 18-signal AI lead scoring · Aadhaar/marksheet/birth-cert OCR · doc verification in 60s · conversion funnel analytics · admission microsite builder · automated follow-up.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** **beta**; "3.2× fill rate" is unverified marketing. Aadhaar OCR + minors' docs = **DPDP Act consent/compliance risk** — a competitor that does privacy-by-design here can market trust as a wedge.

### 2.4 Goood Morning — face-recognition attendance  *(live, flagship)*
**Does:** biometric attendance, "99.7% accurate, <2s." Target: principals, parents, teachers.
**Features `[site]`:** sub-2s face match · anti-spoof liveness · WhatsApp+SMS parent alerts · daily/weekly/monthly reports · live attendance + gate alerts.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** **Google Play install data = 100+ installs / 10 reviews** (see research file) — real usage is tiny vs "250 schools." Face-biometrics of minors = **heavy DPDP/consent exposure**; some schools/parents reject it. A competitor can offer **QR/RFID/app-tap attendance** as a lower-friction, lower-legal-risk alternative and win the privacy-cautious segment.

### 2.5 Bird AI — outbound voice agent  `[beta]`
**Does:** AI caller for admissions/fees/surveys, "8+ Indian languages." Target: founders, finance.
**Features `[site]`:** 8+ Indian-language voices · natural speech · Nucleus+QuickEnroll integration · full transcripts + sentiment · 24/7 at 1/10th call-centre cost · fee-reminder + admission calls.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** **beta**; novelty feature, not a buying decision driver. Voice-AI quality in Indian languages is inconsistent; a bad robocall damages school reputation. Low priority to copy — **let them burn effort here.**

### 2.6 Discover — school-finder for parents
**Does:** school discovery/marketplace for parents (thin detail on site).
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** marketplace only matters at scale (needs many schools). With ~100+ real schools it's **empty-shelf** — ignore until you have density.

## 3. Pricing & packaging  `[site]`
| Tier | Target | Price | Includes | Onboarding/support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter Suite** | <500 students | **₹40/student/mo** (₹480 upfront/12mo; ₹48 standard) | Nucleus + **1** AI module | 10-day onboarding, email+WhatsApp, 30-day pilot, no setup fee |
| **Growth Suite** *(most popular)* | 500–2,000 | **₹100/student/mo** (₹120 standard) | Nucleus + **3** AI modules + Bird minutes | 5-day priority onboarding, dedicated CSM, API access, quarterly QBR |
| **Enterprise Suite** | chains, 2,000+ | **Custom** | All 5 products + Discover, multi-branch, SAML SSO, custom RBAC | White-glove, 24×7 SLA, on-prem/dedicated tenant, SOC2/ISO |

- Bundle = **"20–30% less than piecemeal."** Prices exclude GST. ~**₹3–5 lakh/yr for a 1,000-student school**.
- Onboarding: Nucleus 7–10 days; +3–5 days per added module; ~1 month for 3-product rollout.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** per-student pricing punishes big schools (1,000 students × ₹100 = ₹1L/mo). **Flat-per-school or per-teacher pricing** could undercut on large schools. Free-forever attendance tier as a **land-and-expand wedge** attacks their paid-Starter entry point. 10-day onboarding is slow — **same-day self-serve onboarding** is a real edge for low-fee schools with no IT staff.

## 4. GTM / distribution — partner-commission program  `[site]`
The **real growth engine** (vs founder-led sales). Recurring commission, 4 tiers:
| Tier | Commission | Example monthly business → earnings |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 10% | ₹2L → ₹20k |
| Silver | 15% | ₹5L → ₹75k |
| Gold | 25% | ₹10L → ₹2.5L |
| Platinum | 40% | ₹50L → ₹20L |
- "Up to **40% recurring commission for life**," weekly/monthly bank payout.
- **Zero investment**, 2-min apply, free 2–3hr certification, "earn in under 2 weeks."
- Target partners: **ex-teachers, sales pros, freelancers, homemakers, edu consultants** w/ school networks.
- Split: Inforida handles demo/onboarding/support/product; partner just acquires.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** commission-reseller GTM is **cheap to start but unpredictable and hard to control quality** (see research: no founder w/ proven B2B-edu direct sales). Up to 40% recurring = **thin unit economics**; heavy channel dependence. A competitor with **direct founder-led sales + tight reference-account playbook** can out-execute on trust and retention. Also: **you can recruit their frustrated partners** — same script, better product/payout.

## 5. Platform, architecture & compliance  `[site]` (table-stakes to match)
- **Arch:** single Postgres tenant per school (no data duplication), SAML SSO, RBAC across modules, realtime event bus ("Goood Morning fires → Nucleus updates → Bird calls"), India-region, encrypted at rest, audit-logged.
- **Compliance claims:** SOC 2 Type II (annual), ISO 27001, GDPR-ready (EU+India residency), **DPDP Act 2023 compliant**, AES-256 (rest+transit), SAML SSO enterprise auth. 99.9% uptime.
> **⇒ Competitor angle:** these certs are **expensive and slow** to obtain — if Inforida (a Mar-2025 entity) genuinely holds SOC2 Type II + ISO 27001 already, verify it (auditors publish); **claims may be aspirational** `[unverified]`. For your startup: **DPDP-compliant handling of minors' data is the marketable trust wedge**, especially given their face-biometric exposure.

## 7. Competitive-opportunity summary — where to attack
1. **Beta risk:** Orbit / QuickEnroll / Bird are `[beta]`. Ship *one* of these fully-baked and deeper → beat the "broad but half-finished" suite.
2. **Traction is thin & inflated:** 100+ installs vs "250 schools / 500k–1.2M students." Market is **wide open** — they have no real lock-in.
3. **Privacy wedge:** face-biometrics of minors + Aadhaar OCR = DPDP exposure. Offer privacy-by-design / non-biometric attendance → win cautious schools + parents.
4. **Pricing wedge:** per-student model punishes large schools; slow 10-day onboarding. Undercut with flat pricing + same-day self-serve + free attendance tier.
5. **Vernacular depth:** their AI leans on generic LLMs (exposed to free ChatGPT). Own **deep Hindi/regional + board-aligned** quality on low-end Android — the one moat free tools can't easily match.
6. **Distribution:** they lean on a commission-reseller channel (thin economics, quality-control risk, no proven direct B2B-edu sales). Out-execute with founder-led sales + reference accounts; poach their partners.
7. **Credibility:** narrative-vs-registry gaps (Mar-2025 incorporation, Lucknow, unfunded, self-contradicting student counts). A competitor that is **transparent and verifiable** wins investor + principal trust by contrast.
